##  Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte (OIBSIP) Data Science Internship**.

The project focuses on performing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a retail sales dataset. The analysis aims to understand sales performance, customer demographics, product demand, revenue distribution, and relationships between important numerical variables.

The findings can help a retail business make better decisions regarding **inventory management, marketing, customer targeting, and sales strategies**.

---

##  Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Understand the structure and characteristics of the retail sales dataset.
* Inspect and clean the available data.
* Identify missing values and duplicate records.
* Calculate descriptive statistics.
* Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
* Understand customer age-group distribution.
* Analyze customer gender distribution.
* Identify the top-selling products.
* Analyze revenue generated by product category.
* Explore correlations between numerical variables.
* Generate useful business insights and recommendations.

---

##  Dataset

The project uses a retail sales dataset containing information about customer transactions.

### Key Variables

| Column             | Description                            |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| `Transaction ID`   | Unique identifier for each transaction |
| `Date`             | Date on which the transaction occurred |
| `Age`              | Age of the customer                    |
| `Gender`           | Gender of the customer                 |
| `Product Category` | Category of the purchased product      |
| `Quantity`         | Number of items purchased              |
| `Price per Unit`   | Price of one unit of the product       |
| `Total Amount`     | Total value of the transaction         |

##  Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Loading and Inspection

The dataset was loaded into a Pandas DataFrame and inspected to understand:

* Number of rows and columns
* Column names
* Data types
* Missing values
* Duplicate records
* General dataset structure

### 2. Data Cleaning

Several preprocessing steps were performed, including:

* Removing unnecessary spaces from column names.
* Standardizing categorical values.
* Removing commas from numeric values where necessary.
* Converting numeric columns to appropriate data types.
* Converting the date column into a proper datetime format.
* Checking for missing and invalid values.

### 3. Descriptive Statistics

Statistical measures were calculated for numerical variables, including:

* Mean
* Median
* Mode
* Standard deviation

These statistics help provide an overview of customer and sales behavior.

### 4. Monthly Sales Analysis

Monthly sales were calculated and visualized to identify:

* Sales growth and decline
* High-performing months
* Low-performing months
* Possible seasonal patterns

### 5. Quarterly Sales Analysis

Sales were grouped into quarters to provide a broader view of business performance throughout the year.

### 6. Customer Age Analysis

Customers were divided into meaningful age groups:

* Under 18
* 18–24
* 25–34
* 35–44
* 45–54
* 55–64
* 65+

This helps identify the major customer segments.

### 7. Gender Analysis

Customer transactions were analyzed based on gender to understand customer distribution and sales contribution.

### 8. Top-Selling Products

The analysis identified the **top 10 products based on quantity sold**.

This information can help businesses prioritize products with high customer demand.

### 9. Revenue by Product Category

Total revenue was calculated for each product category to identify the categories generating the highest sales.

### 10. Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was created to examine relationships between:

* Age
* Quantity
* Price per Unit
* Total Amount

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis provides several useful observations:

* Sales performance changes across different months and quarters.
* Some customer age groups represent a larger proportion of the customer base.
* Product demand varies significantly between products.
* Some product categories contribute more revenue than others.
* Customer demographics can provide useful information for targeted marketing.
* The relationship between quantity, price, and total transaction value can help explain sales behavior.
* The difference between the mean and median transaction amount suggests that some transactions have considerably higher values than typical transactions.

---

##  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

### 1. Improve Inventory Management

Maintain sufficient stock for the best-selling products to reduce stock shortages and missed sales opportunities.

### 2. Target Major Customer Segments

Marketing campaigns should focus on the age groups and customer segments that generate the highest purchasing activity.

### 3. Promote High-Performing Categories

Businesses should continue investing in product categories that generate strong revenue while investigating opportunities to improve weaker categories.

### 4. Use Sales Trends for Planning

Monthly and quarterly sales trends can be used to improve inventory planning, promotional campaigns, and resource allocation.

### 5. Investigate High-Value Transactions

Large transactions should be analyzed further to understand what products, customers, or purchasing patterns contribute to unusually high sales.
